# ask
= The Agent
? what can you do
? what can I ask
? help
? how does the agent work
? what is the agent
! Ask a question about Mininotes, or ask for something to be done. I can tell you what is new, read your todo list, add a line to it or to any note ("add milk to Grocery"), share a note or a folder with somebody ("share Grocery with Carol"), add someone, open or find a note, make a new note, and put things in the dock or in your favourites.
! Everything happens on this device: nothing you type here is sent anywhere. Before I change anything that reaches other people, I show you what I am about to do and wait for you to confirm.

# dock
= The dock
? what is the dock
? how do I use the dock
? add to the dock
? remove from the dock
? the row of icons at the bottom
! The dock is the row of icons at the foot of Home, to reach notes and folders in one tap. Add one from its menu with Add to the dock, or carry it onto the dock. Remove from the dock takes it out of the dock only: nothing is deleted, and a favourite stays a favourite.
! Anything you make a favourite also goes into the dock. After that the two are separate. The dock holds as many as you like: when they do not fit, scroll it sideways (swipe on the phone, the wheel or the thin bar on the PC). Each device has its own dock.

# favourites
= Favourites
? what are favourites
? add to favourites
? remove from favourites
? where are my favourites
! Favourites are the notes and folders you want at hand. Add to favourites is in each one's menu. They are all listed in the Favourites icon on Home, in the order you drag them into. A new favourite also goes into the dock; removing a favourite does not take it out of the dock.

# share
= Sharing a note or a folder
? how do I share a note
? share a folder
? share with someone
? how to send a note to someone
! Open the note or folder's menu and go to Sync and sharing, then Share. Pick people from your contacts, or a whole group, and choose what each may do: Can read, Can write, or Admin. They are asked whether to accept it. From then on it keeps itself up to date both ways: what each of you writes is merged.
! You can also ask me: "share Grocery with Carol", or "share Grocery with Carol read only".
> SHARE

# roles
= Who may do what
? what is an owner
? what is an admin
? can write can read
? why can I not change the role
? change the rights
? roles
! Owner: whoever made the thing. Can do everything, including changing anyone's role and removing anyone. An owner's role never changes.
! Admin: can share it on and change the roles of those who can write or read. Can write: reads and writes in it. Can read: reads it, and what they type is not sent.
! Roles are changed in the Share box (Who has access) or on a person's page, from the drop-down beside each thing. Where there is no drop-down, the line under it says why: only the owner or an admin can change it.

# marks
= The sync marks
? what does the mark mean
? what does the tick mean
? green tick
? amber arrow
? red exclamation mark
? grey ring
? is my note synced
! The mark on a note, a folder or a tile says where it stands with the devices it reaches. An empty grey ring: only on this device, not shared. An amber arrow: changes waiting to go; press it to send now. Three amber dots: sent, waiting for the others to say they have it. A green tick: everybody has this version, files included. Two grey bars: paused, this device is not taking in their changes. A red exclamation mark: a device has not been heard from for more than three days while something waits for it.

# notsyncing
= A note does not sync
? my note is not syncing
? not syncing
? why does it not sync
? the other device does not get my changes
! First look at the mark: amber means it is on its way, red means a device has not been reached for days. Mininotes keeps trying by itself. Open Mininotes on the other device, connected to the internet, and leave it a moment: what waits is sent and answered.
! If a strip at the top of the app names a contact in trouble, press What to do: it says what is wrong, what Mininotes does about it, and what you can do. Sync now, in Sync and sharing, sends at once.

# trouble
= The strip at the top
? red strip at the top
? contact in trouble
? what to do strip
! When something is wrong with a contact, for example a device not reached for days, one that is locked, or one whose Mininotes is too old, a strip says so at the top of the app. Mininotes tries again by itself; What to do explains what is wrong and what you can do about it.

# connected
= Connected, offline, encrypted
? what does connected mean
? offline
? what does encrypted mean
? chiffre
? connection
! Connected means this device has found a way to reach your contacts: through the relays, or directly on the same network. Offline means it has not yet; it keeps retrying. Encrypted (Chiffré) means your notebook is locked with your password on this device. Every note is also sealed end to end on its way to another device, whatever this says. Click either word to see the details.

# lock
= Locking and encryption
? how do I lock mininotes
? password
? encrypt my notes
? recovery words
! In Settings, Security, Lock Mininotes encrypts the notebook, its files and your backups on this device. It then opens with your password (or your fingerprint or screen lock on the phone), and 12 recovery words are a spare key. Keep those words somewhere safe: without the password or the words, nobody can open the notebook, not even us.
@ SECURITY

# relays
= Relays and going direct
? what are relays
? does it need internet
? same wifi
? how does it travel
! Notes travel between devices sealed end to end, through Maxima relays, or straight from door to door when both devices are on the same network. The relays only pass on sealed messages they cannot read. Connection details are in your Profile.

# filelink
= Sharing a file through Mininotes
? share a file through mininotes
? linked file
? spreadsheet sync
? keep a file in sync
? ods docx sync
! On the PC, Share a file through Mininotes (in Sync and sharing) links a file that stays where it is on your PC and opens in its own program, for example a spreadsheet in LibreOffice. Each time you save it, that version goes to your other devices, and a version saved elsewhere is written back into it.
! What travels is the whole file, the latest version: changes inside a spreadsheet or a document are not merged. If it changed here and elsewhere before they met, Mininotes asks which version to keep and replaces nothing until you answer. Remove from Mininotes puts the link in the bin on this PC only; the file itself is never deleted.

! Your notes are kept on your devices. They go only to the people and devices you share them with, sealed end to end on the way. There is no account and no server that keeps them. The Agent itself runs on this device and sends nothing.

# stamp
= Stamping on Minima
? stamp a file
? prove a file existed
? integritas
? timestamp on the blockchain
? verify a stamp
? where do I put my integritas api key
? what did I stamp
! Stamp on Minima, in a file's menu or a note's, records the file's fingerprint (never the file) on the Minima blockchain with the date, through your own Integritas account. Later, Verify on Minima finds that record and gives a PDF report, which proves the file existed then, unchanged: even one changed letter makes another fingerprint.
! Link your account once in My profile, under Integritas: paste the API key from your profile at integritas.technology. It is kept in your notebook on the device, locked with it, and sent only to Integritas. In the attachments menu you can stamp several files at once.
@ INTEGRITAS
@ STAMPED
